# Aftergraph Brand Architecture

Aftergraph is the master brand. Repositories, specifications, and services do not become independent brands merely because they have names.

## Classes

| Class | Treatment | Identity entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate | `Aftergraph` | Master mark, wordmark, corporate lockups |
| Product | `AFTERGRAPH / PRODUCT` | Endorsed lockup, product motif, limited accent, real screenshots |
| Technology | `Aftergraph · Technology` | Typed name plus family icon; no autonomous logo |
| Research | `Aftergraph Research / Program` | Research masthead, maturity label, evidence class |
| Internal | Repository name only | No marketing identity or social campaign assets |

## Current registry decisions

The machine-readable authority is [`registry.json`](./registry.json). A classification in that file describes visual treatment only; it does not transfer runtime, scientific, or governance authority.

Sentinel is `needs-review` because naming and trademark collision issues are open. No final endorsed product lockup may ship until that state changes through the repository's governance process.

## Endorsement syntax

- Preferred compact product form: `AFTERGRAPH / WORK INTELLIGENCE`
- Narrative form: `Work Intelligence by Aftergraph`
- Technology form: `Aftergraph Trust Gateway`
- Research form: `Aftergraph Research — AIE (Research Draft)`

Do not use `AVC`, `Autonomous Venture Company`, or `ABDE Intelligence` as a current public parent brand. Historical records may retain those terms when explicitly marked as provenance.
