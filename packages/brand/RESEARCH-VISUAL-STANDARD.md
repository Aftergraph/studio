# Aftergraph Research Visual Standard

Research surfaces use Evidence White as the primary page, Source Serif 4 for editorial headings, Inter for explanation, and JetBrains Mono for identifiers and provenance.

## Evidence hierarchy

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| E0 | Researcher intuition |
| E1 | Secondary commentary / industry evidence |
| E2 | Scholarly preprint |
| E3 | Peer-reviewed research |
| E4 | Standards, official specifications, or benchmark organizations |
| E5 | Independent reproduction or converging empirical evidence |
| E6 | Preregistered reproducible evidence with independent reproduction |

Evidence class is not certification. Each visual must identify scope, source revision, date, and whether evidence came from simulation, live runtime, or independent reproduction.

AIE is visually labelled `RESEARCH DRAFT`; MISSION-Bench results from controlled sandbox simulations retain the visible label `CONTROLLED SIMULATION` unless a different canonical evidence record proves otherwise.
